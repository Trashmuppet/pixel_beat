# Design Bible

## Identity
Minimal.
Monochrome.
Mechanical.
Playful.

## Visual Language
Strict 1-bit graphics.
No gradients.
No transparency.
No anti-aliasing.
Integer scaling only.

## Animation
Driven exclusively by musical events.
240 Hz deterministic simulation.
Renderer never owns timing.

## Product Feel
Fast startup.
Immediate interaction.
Offline ownership.
No accounts.
No ads.
No subscriptions.
One-time Pro unlock (£1.99).

## Non-goals
Do not evolve into a DAW.
Do not imitate skeuomorphic hardware.
Avoid unnecessary controls.
