# Monochrome Beat — Product Vision

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Frozen Vision

## Purpose
Monochrome Beat is an offline-first Android application that allows anyone to create drum patterns and export synchronised monochrome pixel-art music videos in minutes.

The product deliberately avoids becoming a traditional Digital Audio Workstation (DAW). It is a focused creative instrument.

## Vision Statement

Create a beat.
Watch it come alive.
Export it.
Own it.

## Product Goals

- Offline-first operation.
- Target Android 16 (API 36).
- Deterministic audio and visuals.
- One-tap export to MP4, GIF and WAV.
- Zero accounts.
- Zero cloud dependency.
- Zero subscriptions.
- One-time Google Play Pro unlock (£1.99).

## Success Criteria

- New users create a beat in under two minutes.
- Exports are deterministic.
- App starts quickly.
- Runs entirely offline after installation.

## Non-goals

- Recording studio.
- MIDI workstation.
- Cloud collaboration.
- AI music generation.
- Social network.

## Product Identity

Fast.
Minimal.
Monochrome.
Deterministic.
Offline.
