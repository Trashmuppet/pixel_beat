# Product Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Offline First
Everything required to create, edit and export projects works without the Internet.

## Pillar 2 — Export First
The offline renderer is the reference implementation.

## Pillar 3 — Audio Owns Time
Rendering never controls musical timing.

## Pillar 4 — 1-bit Visual Language
Only black (#000000) and white (#FFFFFF).

No gradients.
No transparency.
No anti-aliasing.

## Pillar 5 — Ownership
Projects are portable .mbeat documents.

## Pillar 6 — Simplicity
Every feature must make beat creation faster or exports better.

## Pillar 7 — Sustainable Monetisation
One-time Google Play Pro unlock (£1.99).
No ads.
No subscriptions.
No accounts.
